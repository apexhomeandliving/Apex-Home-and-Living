🚚 Pay On Delivery & Shipping Policy

How does Pay-On-Delivery work?
You do not pay a single kobo online when placing your order. We dispatch your item directly to your doorstep via our courier partners (GIGL / Speedaf). When the courier arrives, you inspect your package first to ensure it matches your order, and then pay cash or make a bank transfer directly to the delivery agent.
How long does delivery take across Nigeria?
• Lagos & Abuja: 24 to 48 Hours.
• Other States & Regional Towns: 3 to 5 Business Days.

🛡️ Quality Guarantee & Inspection

Can I inspect the package before paying?
Yes, 100%! We strictly require all our courier agents to allow you to inspect the physical condition of the item before making payment.
What if I receive a damaged or incorrect item?
If the item is damaged in transit or doesn't match what you ordered, simply reject the package with the courier driver at zero cost to you, and notify our support team immediately.
